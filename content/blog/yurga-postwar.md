**After the war, Yurga grew together with the factory.**

City status:
**1949** — city of district subordination,
**1953** — regional.

The machine-building plant became a city-forming giant.
In parallel, the trust **"Yurgapromstroy"** built:
schools, cultural centers, cinema, medical facilities, sports complexes, residential quarters.

In the 1960s, large-panel construction boom begins, the city becomes modern.
And in the **1960s**, a second giant rises — the **abrasive factory**.

*Series finale — about the crisis of the 90s and today's character of the city.*
