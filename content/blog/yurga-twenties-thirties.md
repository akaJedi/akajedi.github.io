**The 1920s — Yurga learns to live anew.**

Subbotniks, wagon repairs, help for starving Volga region, evening schools, libraries.
Komsomol — the engine of new life.

NEP revives trade.
Then comes collectivization: collective farms multiply, nearby are Siblag territories, camps, repressions of 1937.

Yurga — part of a large country, so its history during this period is just as sharp and difficult.
