**Yurga station opened in 1906.**

But the siding plan was approved back in **1902**:
4 tracks — main, passenger, passing, and military.

From 1906, Yurga became a railway settlement.
Steam locomotives of series "O", "S", "U", "E" worked here — reliable "workhorses" of the Siberian railways.

The station meant people, goods, movement.
Yurga began to rapidly expand on both sides of the tracks.
