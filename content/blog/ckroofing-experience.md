At CK Roofing, I stepped out of the IT world and into a fast-moving service business — bringing my tech skills with me to improve day-to-day operations, automate client workflows, and support both office and field teams.

What started as a temporary pivot turned into an intense and valuable learning experience that sharpened my customer communication, team collaboration, and operational mindset.

<!--more-->

## Key Contributions

**Implemented a CRM system** to replace manual Excel-based lead tracking, improving visibility and response times

**Set up email automation** to streamline lead follow-up and appointment reminders

**Assisted in digital transformation** of client intake and project management workflows

**Learned and applied roofing project knowledge**, including estimating, terminology, and visual planning

**Used new industry-specific software** to draw roof plans and communicate project details

**Managed incoming client calls**, developed frontline communication skills, and improved customer trust and satisfaction

**Coordinated day-to-day team schedules**, contractor timelines, and client expectations in real time

## What I Gained

**Rapid adaptability** to a new industry, new tools, and customer-facing responsibilities

**Stronger ability** to translate technical skills into real-world service improvements

**Deeper understanding** of teamwork, trust, and communication under pressure

**A fresh perspective** on how systems support both people and performance — in any industry

## Reflection

From middle of September to the end of November, I worked as a coordinator in roofing — and it opened my eyes. Even my part of the job was demanding, but watching the crews work gave me real respect for this trade.

Roofing is tough, honest work that requires discipline and responsibility every single day.

Thank you to everyone I worked with. I'm grateful for the experience.

---

*This experience reinforced my belief that technology serves people, not the other way around. Whether in IT infrastructure or service operations, the goal is the same: make things work better, faster, and more reliably for the people who depend on them.*
