**The name "Yurga" is a mystery with several answers.**

There were many versions:

* Selkup — "fat river" (in spring the banks turned yellow from buttercups);
* "pacer" or "small horse trot" — in Tatar interpretations;
* and the most convincing modern version: **Turkic origin, "fast river".**

Anyone who has seen Yurginka in spring understands: this name perfectly captures its character.
Lively, noisy, "talkative" — the river gave its name to the place, and the place to the future city.
