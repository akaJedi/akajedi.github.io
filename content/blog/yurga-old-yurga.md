**The first houses of Yurga were offshoots of the village of Polomoshnoye.**

Approximately **1875–1876** (officially — 1886).
People were drawn here by the fertile banks of the Yurga River.

The **Berezovsky brothers** — Alexander and Grigory — built the first houses.
According to the 1901 census, there were about 40 residents. Few, but this was already the beginning of a real settlement.

Yurga grew quietly, like many Siberian settlements…
until an event occurred nearby that would change its fate forever.
