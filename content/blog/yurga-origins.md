**Yurga is older than it seems.**

Before the city, before the station, before the factories — there was a small Russian settlement called **Ust-Iskitim**. Late XVII century. Quiet life by the Tom River, surrounded by rivers, forests, and fertile banks.

The location was convenient: water nearby, a road along the river, good land. Back then, no one could imagine that in a couple of centuries, an industrial city known throughout Kuzbass would rise here.

Yurga as a settlement began its journey right here — with a small name on a great river.
