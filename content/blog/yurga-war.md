**1940 — construction of the factory began.**

And in 1941, evacuation trains arrived in Yurga: workers and equipment from Stalingrad, Leningrad, Moscow, Kramatorsk.

They built in barracks, without roads, in winter hands stuck to metal — but they launched the factory.
**February 6, 1943** — the machine-building plant was officially put into operation.

In **June 1944**, the first products were produced, tested at the proving ground, and sent to the front.
Yurga became a worker city and a soldier city:
12 thousand went to the front, more than 5.5 thousand did not return.
