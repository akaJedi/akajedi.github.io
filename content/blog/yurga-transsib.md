**Without the railway, Yurga might have remained a village.**

But the **Trans-Siberian Railway** passed nearby — and this became a turning point.

1896. The great road connects the country, and along with it changes the fates of cities.
Yurga became part of this line — and from then on, its life was connected with rails, the station, and movement east and west.

The museum preserves a rail from that time — as a symbol of the point where a different scale began.
