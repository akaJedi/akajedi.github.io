The work was part of a social and charitable program supporting people with disabilities and those affected by the Chernobyl disaster, combining technical expertise with community service.

Key contributions:

 Accepted and processed non-ferrous metals from public

 Identified material types, assessed quality, and calculated purchase values

 Managed receiving, storage, and transfer of materials

 Handled cash operations as financially accountable employee

 Supported charitable program for people with disabilities and Chernobyl disaster victims
